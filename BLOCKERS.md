# BLOCKERS

- **B-001 (2026-10-24) — No robot arm.** Fallback: simulation only (D-001). Impact: no `real` results. M1 real bring-up, the M2 teleop baseline, and all real evaluation are `awaiting-human` with scripts in HUMAN_TODO.md / SESSION_QUEUE.md.
- **B-002 (2026-10-24) — No NVIDIA GPU (Apple M5 Pro, MPS).** Fallback: small ACT / Diffusion Policy on low-dim observations. Impact: the `−VLA pretraining` ablation and VLA fine-tuning (SmolVLA/OpenVLA/π0-class) are not run. They are listed as future work, not as results.
- **B-003 (2026-10-24) — No phone videos yet.** Fallback: synthetic phone videos rendered in the digital twin with a human-hand proxy and coloured fingertip caps. Hand keypoints come from colour blobs plus depth, a stand-in for a HaMeR/WiLoR-class estimator. Impact: M3 perception metrics are `sim`. Real-video perception accuracy is unmeasured until the filming session (HUMAN_TODO H5).
- **B-004 (2026-10-27) — Towel folding (chore 5) cannot be simulated.** The MuJoCo twin has no deformable cloth model with the fidelity needed for a fold, and building one is out of scope. Fallback: report "not run" in sim. The real session S6 is queued (awaiting-human). Impact: no folding claim of any kind.
- **B-005 (2026-10-27) — Real-video reader / hand-pose model not exercised.** No phone clips exist (B-003), so `perception/real_video.py`-style ingestion and a HaMeR/WiLoR-class estimator are untested. The coloured fingertip-cap detector (`hand_source="caps"`) is the real-world fallback and is evaluated in sim (M3 sweep). Impact: M3 numbers are sim only, and the first real clips may need reader fixes (HUMAN_TODO H5/H6).
- **B-006 (2026-10-27) — Diffusion Policy does not reach a usable policy within the shared 3000-step budget** (≤ 10 % per object on validation, D-026). Fallback: ACT is the primary policy class for every method. DP is kept in the code, and an action-spread uncertainty signal is wired up but not used in headline results. Impact: the "action-distribution entropy" signal of Contribution 2 is not evaluated; ensemble disagreement is.
