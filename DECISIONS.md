# DECISIONS

Format: `D-NNN (date) — decision. Rationale. Impact on claims.`

- **D-001 (2026-10-24) — Fallback rungs: sim-only robot, Apple MPS/CPU compute, synthetic phone videos.** No arm, no NVIDIA GPU, and no phone videos exist yet. Rationale: the research question can still be posed and answered at the `sim` stage. Impact: every headline number is labelled `sim`. The video-vs-teleop and active-request answers are *simulation evidence only* until the sessions in SESSION_QUEUE.md are run.
- **D-002 (2026-10-24) — Own SO-100-class MJCF instead of a downloaded model.** Link lengths approximate the SO-100 (0.113 m / 0.135 m / 0.10 m TCP), with a 5-DoF chain + parallel jaw and a closed-form IK (`common/kinematics.py`). Rationale: self-contained, testable, and no licence/network dependency. Impact: sim kinematics ≠ a calibrated real unit. Real link lengths come from calibration (M1).
- **D-003 (2026-10-24) — Grasp = physical finger contact + kinematic attachment.** Fingers collide with objects, so imprecise approaches push objects. A grasp only *holds* when the object passes a geometric jaw-containment test at closing time (`TidyEnv.grasp_check`). Rationale: reliable grasp physics in MuJoCo needs heavy tuning, and this standard shortcut keeps grasp *precision* (position, yaw, width) as the thing being tested. Impact: sim success overestimates real success for slippery/heavy objects. Grasp-force failures are not modelled.
- **D-004 (2026-10-24) — Gravity compensation on arm links in sim.** Real STS3215-class servos hold pose under gravity with an internal PID. Without gravcomp our position servos sagged ~1.7 cm at full reach. Impact: none on claims. Real sag is measured in the repeatability test (M1).
- **D-005 (2026-10-24) — Policies use low-dimensional, object-centric observations** (proprio + perceived target-object pose + place-target pose, all relative to the TCP), not raw images. Rationale: CPU/MPS budget, 10-demo data regime, and the same observation is available on the real robot from the overhead camera perception. Impact: results do not test end-to-end visuomotor learning. Perception errors enter through measured noise (M3) and are swept.
- **D-006 (2026-10-24) — Action space: TCP position delta + jaw yaw (π-symmetric) + tool pitch + gripper, 10 Hz, chunked.** IK and the safety filter run below the policy, so a learned policy can never command a joint outside the safety limits.
- **D-007 (2026-10-24) — The scripted controller (`sim/expert.py`) is used only as a *simulated teleoperator* and for env tests.** It is never reported as a learned policy.
- **D-008 (2026-10-24) — Grasp hold switched from kinematic teleport to a soft weld (activated at the jaw-containment test). Free-object horizontal speed is clamped to 0.35 m/s and angular speed to 10 rad/s. Rolling friction is on (condim 6).** Rationale: the kinematic teleport, and squeezing light objects between a stiff servo and the table, ejected objects at unphysical speeds (traced: a cup slid 1.6 m). Impact: sim cannot reproduce fast tumbling. It slightly under-states "knocked off the table" failures.
- **D-009 (2026-10-24) — Continuous gripper opening command.** Demonstrators and the teleoperator open the jaw only ~2–3.5 cm wider than the object, not fully. Rationale: a fully opened jaw (8 cm) knocked neighbouring items off the shelf/bin, and careful humans and operators don't do this. Video retargeting gets the width directly from the thumb–index distance.
- **D-010 (2026-10-24) — Scripted/teleop motion rises vertically to a 12 cm transit height before horizontal moves, and moves in (radius, pan, z).** Rationale: traced failures where the fingers swept through just-placed items, and straight lines crossed the base no-go column (correctly held by the safety filter → timeouts).
- **D-011 (2026-10-24) — Workspace fixtures resized before any result was produced:** bins 15×12 cm (the 11.4 cm pen did not fit the old bin), shelf lowered to 2.5 cm with shorter items, tape radius 3.0 cm, and per-item drop spots in bins. The evaluation protocol was re-frozen after these changes. **No result uses an earlier protocol** (checked: results/ contains only the safety-smoke log).
- **D-012 (2026-10-24) — Simulated-teleop feasibility (20 eval_seen trials, not a headline result):** desk 16/20, sorting 19/20, shelf 15/20, stacking 14/20, tray 16/20. This is the reference ceiling for a careful operator in this twin.
- **D-013 (2026-10-24) — Synthetic human demonstrator and simulated hand estimator (B-003 fallback).** Human motion = minimum-jerk segments (peak 0.4–0.9 m/s), oblique approach from the human side (25–60° from vertical), pinch across the narrow dimension, 1–2 mm tremor, handheld-phone shake. The 3D hand keypoints used for learning come from a *simulated* HaMeR/WiLoR-class estimator: GT keypoints + AR(1) camera-frame noise (σ_xy 6 mm, σ_z 12 mm), emitted only on frames where skin pixels are visible. The noise level is an assumption, so M3/M4 report a sweep. Fingertip-cap colour detection is the alternative `caps` hand source. Impact: M3 numbers are `sim`. Real hand-pose error is unmeasured until filming.
- **D-014 (2026-10-26) — Camera pose by tracked PnP.** Full 4-marker solves where possible, then forward/backward tracking with ≥3 markers matched to the previous pose's projection (jump-gated), then hold-last. Rationale: one mat corner is often occluded by an object or the hand, which left 12–86 % of frames solved; tracking raises it to 80–100 %. The real system uses ArUco IDs, where correspondences come for free.
- **D-015 (2026-10-26) — Release time = first frame at the bottom of the hand's place motion (smoothed grasp-centre height, near the settled object), with aperture opening as the fallback.** Rationale: with σ_z/σ_xy-level keypoint noise the aperture-threshold cue was 0.5 s early to 3 s late. Height-minimum cut the IQR to [−0.07, +0.10] s on 24 sim clips (tuned on `film` clips only, never on eval).
- **D-016 (2026-10-26) — Program inference: landmark positions at place time come from where the other objects came to rest (place position if carried earlier, else rest position), not from occluded frames. Pick order is enforced only by landmark dependencies (A placed on B ⇒ B first) or ≥90 % pairwise precedence agreement over ≥5 demos.** Identical instances (block_red / block_red2) are interchangeable for scoring.
- **D-017 (2026-10-26) — Bug fix in the synthetic demonstrator:** the hand path is planned before rendering, so object-relative goals (stacking) used the landmark object's *start* pose. The demonstrator now tracks where it has put each object. No result had been produced with the bug (results/ held only the safety smoke).
